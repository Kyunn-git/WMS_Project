☕︎ Menu

What's a movie without a snack? Here at Coffee Roll, we offer a variety
of snacks and drinks to accompany your film experience. From sweet treats
to savory bites, our menu is designed to satisfy every craving.

Avocado Toast

Freshly-baked sourdough topped with slices of avocado, cream cheese and a sunny side up egg that will for sure satisfy your cravings for the savoury and the sweet.

Bagel

A freshly-baked bagel with avocado, onions, cheese and a sunny side up egg.

Pancakes

Freshly-made pancakes topped with fruits, butter and maple syrup. For the ones who love a classic sweet breakfast.

Egg & Ham Croissant

A beautifully baked croissant with a scrambled egg and ham inside. A first bite you'll never forget.

Special Fruit Parfait

A delightful layering of fresh fruits, creamy yogurt, and granola in a glass. Perfect for those who love a healthy yet indulgent treat.

Taiyaki Share Plate

Ten pieces of taiyaki, all filled with different fillings of your choice. We offer red bean paste, custard, kaya and chocolate; one for every taste.

Bunny Yogurt Bowl

A freshly made yogurt topped with raspberries, strawberry sauces, bananas and an adorable bunny-shaped chocolate. Don't forget to take a picture!

Chicken Chop

Deep fried chicken thigh with a side of fries. A classic for the meat lovers.
