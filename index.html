Welcome to Coffee Roll! We are film collective that offers daily movie showings in a cozy upstairs cafe. Enjoy old cinema with good company, warm atmosphere, and a curated selection of food and drinks. Whether you're a film fanatic or a watch-party foodie, give us the honor of being the hot chocolate to your movie night!

Sit With Us

Our film club honors a variety of prefenreces. Films are voted to show for the week by the lovely community of filmheads that visit us. Want to see something special? Watch with us and contribute to the next week's film choice!
